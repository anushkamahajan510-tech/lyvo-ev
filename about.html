Section 01

Company Overview

LIVELIFE AUTOTECH PRIVATE LIMITED, established in 2018, focuses on electric mobility solutions for passenger transportation, commercial cargo and waste management. Through its LYVO product range, the company aims to support practical mobility needs with a customer-focused approach.

Operating under the leadership of CMD Mr. Sanjay Mahajan, the company addresses the economic and environmental imperatives of commercial transportation in India. With the corporate tagline "Life, On the Move." and campaign "Move Green. Earn Clean.", LIVELIFE AUTOTECH engineers rugged electric three-wheelers that maximize driver earnings while contributing to cleaner urban air quality.

Section 02

Our Approach to Electric Mobility

Building commercial electric three-wheelers grounded in real operating conditions and total cost of ownership.

01

Purpose-Engineered Chassis

Rather than converting lightweight personal frames, our three-wheelers utilize structural tubular steel chassis designed for heavy commercial loading, rough road impacts, and continuous multi-shift operations.

02

Safe Lithium Battery Chemistry

Equipped with thermal-resilient Lithium Iron Phosphate (LFP) battery technology that provides high cycle life, safe operating characteristics across Indian summers, and an indicative 5-year battery warranty structure.

03

Fast Turnaround Charging

Engineered with 0% to 80% fast charging in 90 minutes so drivers and municipal operators can rapidly recharge during regular turnaround intervals and maintain maximum daily uptime.
