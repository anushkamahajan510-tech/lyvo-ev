Designed for Cleaner Urban Neighborhoods

An electric waste-management vehicle engineered for urban local bodies, smart municipalities, residential gated communities, hospitals, and educational campuses.

01

Segregated Dry & Wet Chambers

Partitioned stainless/galvanized steel body ensures source-segregated collection of dry recyclables and organic wet waste per environmental regulations.

02

100% Leak-Proof Sealed Basin

Watertight containment floor prevents foul leachate spillage onto public streets, preserving hygiene throughout residential morning collection rounds.

03

Easy-Lift Tipping Mechanism

Ergonomic hydraulic/assisted mechanical tipping action enables effortless refuse discharge into primary municipal compactor bins without manual lifting strain.
