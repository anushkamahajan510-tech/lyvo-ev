Strategic Purpose

Vision, Mission & Goals

The corporate compass guiding LIVELIFE AUTOTECH PRIVATE LIMITED as we build sustainable electric vehicle solutions for India.

Corporate Vision

Our Vision for Indian Mobility

"To contribute to a cleaner and more accessible mobility future through practical electric vehicle solutions."

Our vision addresses the everyday challenges of Indian road transport: creating clean, dependable three-wheelers that reduce atmospheric emissions in our cities while providing commercially viable alternatives for drivers, small enterprises, and civic authorities.

Corporate Mission

Our Operational Mission

"To develop customer-focused electric mobility solutions that address the everyday needs of passenger transport, commercial goods movement and waste management."

Through our three flagship platforms—LYVO Passenger 6+1, LYVO Loader, and LYVO Garbage—we translate this mission into everyday reality, focusing on total cost of ownership, vehicle durability, and long battery life.

Key Milestones

Our Five Strategic Goals

01

Build a Recognisable Electric Mobility Brand

Establish LYVO as a trusted, household name synonymous with durable commercial engineering, driver satisfaction, and transparent customer service.

02

Develop a Reliable Customer Support Ecosystem

Build a responsive aftermarket support structure with readily available genuine spare parts, fast technician support, and comprehensive battery lifecycle services.

03

Expand Dealer and Distribution Partnerships

Grow authorized dealership networks across key urban and industrial corridors in Maharashtra, Delhi NCR, Gujarat, UP, Karnataka, and beyond.

04

Support Sustainable Commercial Transportation

Enable small business owners, delivery operators, and municipalities to transition seamlessly from fossil fuel to clean electric propulsion.

05

Continuously Improve the Customer Experience

Incorporate real-world driver feedback, battery analytics, and ergonomic insights to continually refine future iterations of our vehicles.
