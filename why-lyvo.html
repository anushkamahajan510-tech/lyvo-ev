Brand Philosophy

Why LYVO?

Designed Around Real-World Mobility Needs. Engineered to make commercial electric vehicles dependable, affordable to operate, and profitable to own.

01

Purpose-Focused Vehicles

Commercial electric vehicles cannot be adapted from leisure toys. They require structural integrity engineered for heavy daily mileage and dense payloads.

Every LYVO vehicle is purpose-engineered from the ground up: the LYVO Passenger features wide seating ergonomics for 6 commuters; the LYVO Loader incorporates a 750 kg reinforced flatbed with 25% gradeability; and the LYVO Garbage features 100% leak-proof sealed bins for municipal sanitation.

02

Practical Commercial Mobility

Designed for the reality of Indian operating conditions.

From rough urban roads and monsoons to tight market alleys and heavy incline flyovers, LYVO three-wheelers combine heavy-duty hydraulic telescopic suspension, robust drum brakes, and low-speed high-torque electric powertrains to deliver dependable daily commercial runs.

03

One Platform. Multiple Commercial Applications

Supporting the backbone of daily urban logistics and commuting.

The modular architecture supports passenger transport, intra-city parcel logistics, fresh produce distribution, municipal waste tipping, and dedicated campus transport without requiring expensive proprietary tooling.

04

Customer-Focused Support & 5 Year Battery Peace of Mind

A commercial vehicle is an income-generating asset that must stay on the road.

LIVELIFE AUTOTECH focuses on driver and fleet owner uptime through accessible spare parts availability, fast charging capability (0% to 80% in 90 mins), and an indicative 5-year battery warranty structure designed to safeguard operator capital investment.

05

A Commitment to Sustainable Mobility

"Move Green. Earn Clean."

Commercial three-wheelers cover thousands of kilometers every month inside congested urban areas. Electrifying this segment delivers immediate improvements in city air quality and lowers ambient street noise while cutting operating running costs by up to 75% compared to petrol and diesel alternatives.
